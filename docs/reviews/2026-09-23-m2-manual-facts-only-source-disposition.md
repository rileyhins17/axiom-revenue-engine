## Disposition

The narrow route under review is an owner-operated person opening an ordinarily
public page and recording only concise company-level facts in original wording,
with the page URL, observation time, method, confidence, and uncertainty. It
excludes copying page text, HTML, DOM, screenshots, images, personal names,
individual contact details, reviews/testimonials, and form interaction.

The completed identity-research scope permitted manual inspection of
accessible official pages and concise factual notes for selecting candidates;
its search and candidate caps are exhausted and it does not authorize a new
M2 assessment. On the evidence below,
that limited manual route is **source-policy-eligible in principle for eight of
the ten proposed businesses**, subject to a fresh exact-source authorization
and the remaining M2 gates. This is not a site-granted copying licence, a
decision that the terms legally authorize automated collection, or permission
to run an M2 capture or assessment. Codex's delegated identity decisions for
the ten are saved; they do not establish website need. No business has been
assessed and no M2 work is counted by
this review.

Raw HTML retention is **not cleared for any source**. Automated HTTP/browser
fetch is **not cleared by this manual route for any source**. `robots.txt`
allowance or absence of an obvious terms page does not grant either right.
