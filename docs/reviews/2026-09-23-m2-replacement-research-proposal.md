## Exact operation if approved

  and **24 page opens** total. Count failed opens and searches against the caps.
  Stop when two supported replacements and one backup for each position are
  documented, or when a cap is reached. No paid source, account, API, browser

- Use public search as a hint and each business's own accessible site for its
  exact name, official website, actual Waterloo/Cambridge location, service
  category, and company-stated independence. A service-area mention alone does
  not prove location. Do not treat company statements as registry verification.
- Record every retained factual observation with its source URL, observation
  time, method, confidence, and uncertainty. Keep concise notes in ignored
  `data/kw-evaluation/`; do not save page bodies, screenshots, DOM, contact

- Do not log in, solve access challenges, submit forms, contact a business, or
  bypass a 403, robots restriction, paywall, or other access denial. On unclear

  unresolved. Stop the whole operation if the research tool requires spending,
  its terms are unclear, or a count cap is reached.

  all used counts, and any shortfall. A candidate is "supported" for owner
  identity review only when the official site supports the exact city, niche,
  and independence claim. This does not establish website need or consent.

by Git and exists in the working checkout. The search found two

downgraded from the initial research note before any selection.

businesses, and C$0. Three integration-owner verification opens occurred after

cap, but it **did breach the stop-on-any-cap rule** in this proposal. No further
research opens are authorized under this approval. The extra opens only
checked existing candidates and caused the Cambridge downgrade; there was no
capture, login, form submission, contact, provider spend, or outreach.
