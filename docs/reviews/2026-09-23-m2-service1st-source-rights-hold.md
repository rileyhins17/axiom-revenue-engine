# M2 Service 1st source-rights disposition — hold live fetch

**Date:** 2026-09-23 UTC. **Reviewer:** Codex. **Scope:** one read-only policy check for the proposed
Service 1st Heating & Cooling M2 replacement. This is a research disposition,
not source-capture, materialization, assessment, or owner release approval.

## Decision

**HOLD the M2 HTTP fetch and website assessment.** The exact public homepage
and current site-specific conditions have not been cleared for the proposed
automated fetch, and no raw-HTML or screenshot retention permission was found.
Do not turn the site's availability, a robots allowance, or a privacy policy
into a copying licence. Leave `browserCaptureAuthorized` and all contact,
provider, send, and spend authority at zero.

The narrower future design target is transient observation with only factual
company-level findings in our own words, source URLs, capture method/time,
confidence, and audit version retained. Exclude personal names, individual
contact details, testimonials, photos, profiles, and copied page text. This is
a lower-risk design direction, **not** approval to perform the fetch.

## Evidence and limits

- The [company homepage](https://www.service1st.ca/) was accessible during the
  bounded read-only check. It identifies a Cambridge HVAC business and displays
  a copyright notice. The site's [contact page](https://www.service1st.ca/contact/)
  has form-submission privacy language; it does not authorize data collection
  for this project. No form was submitted.
- A separate 15:37 UTC policy check retrieved the site's
  [robots.txt](https://www.service1st.ca/robots.txt) and
  [privacy policy](https://www.service1st.ca/privacy-policy/). It found public
  robots paths but no affirmative HTML-retention permission. A later six-open
  follow-up could not retrieve its guessed robots/privacy URLs through that
  research tool. Recheck the exact live paths at a future gate; neither result
  alone establishes legal rights or current fetch eligibility.
- [CIPO's copyright guide](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canadian-intellectual-property-office/en/guide-copyright)
  explains that facts are not protected as expression, while its
  [infringement guidance](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canadian-intellectual-property-office/en/copyright-infringement)
  addresses copying protected work. The [Copyright Act's reproduction right](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/C-42/section-3.html)
  and [research fair-dealing provision](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/C-42/section-29.html)
  do not by themselves resolve this specific commercial workflow or temporary
  full-page processing.
- The [Office of the Privacy Commissioner's joint scraping statement](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/opc-news/speeches-and-statements/2023/js-dc_20230824/)
  cautions that publicly available personal information remains subject to
  privacy law. This evaluation should not collect individual-level material.

The follow-up used six page-open attempts and ended at approximately
15:47:55 UTC. No site capture, download, body retention, contact, local
database write, or provider action occurred. This document is a conservative
engineering disposition, not a legal opinion.

## Exit condition

Recheck the exact current site's robots and any applicable terms, document a
source-specific facts-only policy decision with a bounded retention rule, and
separately record the M2 capture authorization against the exact immutable
packet before any live fetch. If any condition remains unclear, keep this
business blocked and evaluate another source without broadening the policy by
assumption.
