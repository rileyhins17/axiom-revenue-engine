# M2 public research scope for owner review

**Prepared:** 2026-09-21. **State:** PENDING OWNER DECISION.
**Purpose:** identify the ten real businesses needed for M2. This document is
a proposed research operation, not a completed source review or an execution
authorization. The current repository contains synthetic evaluation records;
no real ten-business source plan or owner-reviewed manifest has been found.

## The proposed operation

Codex may manually inspect public search results and official business pages
for up to **20 independent businesses**, stopping when it can present ten
evidence-backed candidates. The scope is Kitchener, Waterloo and Cambridge,
Ontario, across roofing, HVAC and landscaping. Select at least two businesses
from each city and each niche. Prefer one from each of the nine city/niche
combinations, with the tenth chosen for coverage and evidence quality.

Use at most **12 search queries and 60 page opens** in this initial research
operation. Count unsuccessful opens toward the cap. Use only already available
read-only search/browser tools. There is no paid source, API subscription,
browser service, account creation or marginal engine-provider spend: **C$0**.

Record organization name, official website, public business location, service
category, direct evidence of independent ownership when available, and the
source URL, actual observation time, method and uncertainty for each claim.
Unclear identity, city, niche or ownership remains unconfirmed and excludes
the candidate from the final ten. Service-area text alone does not prove that
the business is located in a city. Avoid national chains and lead-generation
intermediaries. These are research candidates, not qualified leads.

Retain concise factual notes and source links in ignored local evaluation
storage. Do not retain full page bodies, personal contact details, inbox data,
screenshots or downloaded files. Review notes after 30 days; an expiry marker
does not itself delete evidence or authorize a cleanup operation.

Stop on unclear access/retention terms, denied access, a login or paywall,
personal-data-dominant content, contradictory identity evidence, a tool that
requires spending, or either count limit. Do not bypass restrictions. Report
any shortfall and the rejected or unresolved candidates with reasons.

## What the owner receives

One private, readable table of the proposed ten businesses and their source
evidence, plus a count and reason for any exclusions. Riley or Aidan can approve,
reject or correct the exact identities, locations, niches and independence
claims. Codex must not record that identity review as if the owner had already
performed it.

That review allows preparation of the exact manifest and capture packet.
The first real website capture is a later, separately reviewed operation using
the bounded transport, source-policy and retention checks. Local database
setup/migration and assessment writes retain their explicit release gates.
This research decision grants no background crawling, raw HTML retention,
database mutation, provider activation, contact enrichment, qualification,
messages, calls, form submissions, inbox sync, deployment or outreach.
