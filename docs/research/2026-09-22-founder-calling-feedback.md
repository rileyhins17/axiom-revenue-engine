# Founder calling feedback — September 22, 2026

**Source:** [Aidan's internal Slack recap](https://axiominternationalhq.slack.com/archives/D0C0JKBQM8X/p1790103750453269), posted September 22 at 3:02 p.m. Eastern. This is a founder's report of a separate calling/extension workflow, not an Axiom Revenue Engine run. The underlying call recordings, transcripts, extension export, and individual source evidence were not independently inspected for this note. It is private calibration input, not proof of lead quality, consent, or a customer outcome.

Aidan reported 18 worked leads and 18 recorded call attempts in the extension/export snapshot: nine no answer or voicemail, four not interested, two gatekeepers, two marked follow-up, and one uncontacted/capture failure. He also described later manual calls outside that snapshot. The two follow-up labels need review: one conversation established no sales interest or agreed next step, and another has incomplete transcript capture. Do not promote either label to a qualified opportunity from the export alone. He reported no closes.

The strongest commercial finding was that three businesses without useful websites said they were already at or over capacity and did not want more demand. A missing website therefore supports a website observation, not buying appetite or a reason to contact. Aidan also judged small defects—an old copyright date, a typo, directory-hours disagreement, or an isolated placeholder—too weak by themselves to justify a C$900+ website job. His proposed next-batch standard is a plainly poor or unfinished customer-facing site, or no site **plus evidence of growth appetite**, with preference for higher-value owner-operated project work. This is a hypothesis for reviewed targeting and evaluation, not an automatic policy or threshold change.

The message mentions a call to “RoofSaver”: the opening was delivered, followed by a hang-up and no captured business-side speech. That name may match proposed M2 candidate M2-01, Roofsaver Roofworks Inc., but the recap does not bind the call to the M2 source identity or establish a website-quality finding. None of the proposed ten receives an individual identity approval or site label from this message. The earlier aggregate screenshot feedback that most of that batch had decent sites likewise remains unassigned to individuals.
